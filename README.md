A two-line warning to a past self about one specific moment: the university
letter on the kitchen table, opened before Dad gets home — and what that
moment cost.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`
(`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` or `/output`).
